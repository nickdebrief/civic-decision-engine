Privacy notice

Optional analytics status: Google Analytics loading is currently disabled. A saved browser preference does not itself enable Google Analytics; loading requires both a valid affirmative choice and a separate operational enablement. Microsoft Clarity is disabled.

Privacy and user choice

Access to public civic records and public processes on Civic Decision Engine does not depend on tracking or on your analytics choices. Optional analytics are disabled by default. If CDE enables an optional analytics provider, it will load only after your valid affirmative choice and only on the eligible public pages listed below. You can decline or withdraw your choice without losing public access. Withdrawing stops future loading through CDE’s consent controls; it does not undo information already collected by a provider.

Optional purposes and choices

Civic Decision Engine may use Google Analytics only on these query-free public pages: /, /archive, /associations, /collections, /conditions, /conditions/map, /determinations, /stats, /stats/timeline, /traceability, and /transmissions. Query-bearing pages and protected, administrative, API, record/detail, document, artefact, verification, and authentication pages are excluded.

  • Traffic measurement — Google Analytics: measures visits to eligible public pages.

Google Analytics is not used for individual inference, CDE evidence, or a governed determination. You may decline without losing public access. When optional controls are available on an eligible page, use Privacy preferences to choose separately, change, or withdraw a choice. When providers are operationally disabled, the same entry point lets you read this notice and clear a saved local preference; it does not enable a provider.

Local preference

CDE stores your local analytics choice in browser storage under cde_optional_analytics_preferences_v2. A choice is valid for up to 90 days and is treated as expired when CDE reads it; expiry is not a claim that browser storage is automatically deleted at that moment. Withdrawal, malformed data, or a superseded local value clears the locally controllable analytics state and requires a fresh choice. Withdrawing a choice does not delete data already held by a provider.

Provider cookies and storage

If Google Analytics is both selected and operationally enabled, it may use browser storage. Google Analytics is currently disabled, so this notice does not state production cookie names, purposes, domains, paths, Secure/SameSite attributes, or lifetimes that have not been observed for this site.

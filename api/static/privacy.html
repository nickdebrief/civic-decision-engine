Privacy notice

Optional analytics status: Each provider requires both its separate Civic Decision Engine operational switch to be enabled and your separate, valid affirmative choice. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are not required to use the public site.

Optional purposes and choices

Civic Decision Engine offers two separate optional purposes on these query-free public pages only: /, /archive, /associations, /collections, /conditions, /conditions/map, /determinations, /stats, /stats/timeline, /traceability, and /transmissions. Query-bearing pages and protected, administrative, API, record/detail, document, artefact, and authentication pages are excluded.

  • Traffic measurement — Google Analytics: measures visits to eligible public pages.
  • Public-page usability observation — Microsoft Clarity: supports aggregate usability analysis, including heatmaps and session recordings, on eligible public pages.

Neither purpose is used for individual inference, CDE evidence, or a governed determination. A provider remains off unless both its operational switch and its relevant affirmative choice are valid. You may choose either, both, or neither without losing public access. On an eligible page, use Privacy preferences to change or withdraw either choice independently.

Local preference

CDE stores your two local choices in browser storage under cde_optional_analytics_preferences_v2 for up to 90 days. Expiry, withdrawal, malformed data, or a superseded local value clears the locally controllable analytics state and requires a fresh choice. Withdrawing a choice does not delete data already held by a provider.

Provider cookies and storage

After an affirmative Google Analytics choice, Google documents a first-party _ga cookie. After an affirmative Microsoft Clarity choice, Microsoft documents first-party _clck and _clsk cookies and describes possible Microsoft third-party cookies. The exact provider cookie domains, attributes, and lifetimes for this property and project will be confirmed in a controlled pre-activation test. Optional analytics will not be activated until that confirmation is complete.

Retention, access, and deletion

The controller-confirmed Google Analytics user and event retention setting is two months, with reset on new activity off. Microsoft publishes 30-day playback retention and nine-month retention for click data, heatmaps, and labelled or favourited sessions. CDE policy is not to favourite or label Clarity sessions. Provider-held data is subject to the applicable provider process and retention rules; local withdrawal does not retrospectively delete it.

Providers and transfers

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are separate providers. CDE does not describe optional analytics as anonymous or state that provider data is never shared with third parties. Before optional analytics is activated, CDE will confirm the applicable provider terms, processing entities, transfer information, and account-specific deletion route. Current provider documentation is available from Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity.

This notice describes the default-off optional analytics controls prepared for Civic Decision Engine. It does not activate either provider.
